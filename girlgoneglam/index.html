My Story

A glow-up built on small, intentional choices

There was a time when I didn't feel confident. I rarely wore makeup, my posture was terrible, and I talked to myself in ways I never would to a friend.

So I started changing things, slowly and consistently. I learned how to care for my skin, fixed how I carried myself, paid attention to my wardrobe, moved my body with love, and started talking to myself like I mattered.

Girl Gone Glam is where I share everything I've learned, so you feel inspired, seen and ready to start your own glow-up, whatever that looks like for you.

Let's glow, together. — Kerry
